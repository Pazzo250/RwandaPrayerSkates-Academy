Skating Tips for Beginners: Getting Started on Wheels

Published on April 15, 2025 by Rwanda Prayer Skates

Starting your skating journey can be exciting but also intimidating. At Rwanda Prayer Skates Academy, we believe everyone can learn to skate with the right guidance and safety precautions.

Essential Beginner Tips:

  • Wear Protective Gear: Always use helmets, knee pads, and elbow pads to prevent injuries.
  • Start Slow: Begin on flat surfaces and practice balancing before attempting to move.
  • Learn to Fall Safely: Tuck and roll to avoid wrist injuries.
  • Practice Balance: Stand still on skates for a few minutes before gliding.
  • Use Proper Technique: Bend your knees slightly and keep your weight centered.

Remember, practice makes perfect. Join our beginner classes in Kigali to get personalized instruction and build confidence on wheels.
