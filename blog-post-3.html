Community Spotlight: Recent Skating and Biking Events in Kigali

Published on April 10, 2025 by Rwanda Prayer Skates

Our recent events in Kigali have brought together families, friends, and skating enthusiasts for unforgettable experiences. From beginner workshops to competitive races, the community spirit is alive and thriving.

Highlights from Recent Events:

  • Family Skate Day: Over 50 families participated in our monthly skate day, learning together and building bonds.
  • Youth Biking Challenge: Local teens competed in safe biking races, promoting healthy competition and teamwork.
  • Beginner Workshops: New participants learned skating fundamentals in supportive group sessions.
  • Community Picnic: Combined sports with social activities, creating lasting memories for all ages.

These events not only promote physical activity but also foster community connections and personal growth. Join us for our next event and be part of the Rwanda Prayer Skates family!
